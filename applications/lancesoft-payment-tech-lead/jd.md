2. Job Purpose
The Payments Technical Lead is responsible for analyzing, designing, and governing technical changes across all payment applications that are impacted due to the Oracle Banking Payments (OBP) and Core Banking upgrade program payments platforms.
The role works closely with application owners, program leads, and architecture teams to understand the end‑to‑end impact and quantum of change across country‑specific payment rails, gateways, integrations, and downstream payment systems, ensuring all impacted payment applications remain compliant, stable, and aligned across UAE, Egypt, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and India.
4. Key Result Areas
1. Payment Application Impact Analysis & Design
Own the technical impact analysis and design for all payment applications impacted by OBP and Core Banking upgrades.
Prepare and maintain HLDs and LLDs for impacted payment systems, integrations, and country‑specific payment rails.
Translate upgrade‑driven changes into clear technical designs, impact notes, and implementation guidance.
2. Cross‑Application & Stakeholder Coordination
Work closely with all payment application owners, program leads, and architecture teams to:
Understand upgrade‑driven changes
Assess functional and technical impact across payment applications
Estimate the quantum of change across systems and integrations
Act as the single technical coordination point for payment application changes across programs.
3. Country‑Specific Payment Rail Impact Handling
Analyze and design changes across country‑specific payment rails and clearing systems (e.g., UAEFTS, WPS, ICCS, KNPS, QATCH, RTGS, ACH) resulting from OBP and Core Banking upgrades.
Ensure impacted payment applications comply with local scheme rules, message formats, settlement logic, and regulatory requirements.
Support scheme‑related certification and readiness activities for impacted systems.
4. Integration & Downstream System Alignment
Design and review changes across integrations between:
Payment applications
OBP and Core Banking
Channels and digital platforms
Gateways and reconciliation systems (Payment Tracker, Sherlock, Bravos, TLM)
Ensure all impacted integrations remain aligned with enterprise architecture, security, performance, and resilience standards.
5. Design Support During Build, Testing & Release
Support build teams by clarifying design changes and resolving impact‑related gaps.
Assist SIT/UAT by:
Analyzing defects related to upgrade‑driven impacts
Identifying root causes across payment applications and interfaces
Validate readiness of impacted payment applications for release and production deployment.
6. Technical Governance & Consistency
Ensure design consistency and traceability across all payment applications impacted by the upgrade.
Highlight risks, dependencies, and cross‑application impacts to program and senior stakeholders.
Ensure alignment with change management, release governance, and BCP/DR requirements.
