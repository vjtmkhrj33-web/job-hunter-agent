# Pine Labs – Senior Product Manager – Cards, Online Payment Gateway

**Location:** Bengaluru

## Job Description

About the Role
Pine Labs is looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the Cards charter within our Online Payment Gateway
business. You&#39;ll drive the strategy, roadmap, and execution for card-based payment acceptance — covering
debit/credit cards, tokenization, network integrations, and authorization/settlement flows — for merchants
transacting online.
What You&#39;ll Do
• Own the end-to-end product roadmap for Cards within the Online PG stack — acceptance, routing,
tokenization, and checkout experience
• Define and drive success on key metrics: authorization rates, success rates, latency, and cost per transaction
• Work closely with card networks (Visa, Mastercard, Amex etc.), issuing/acquiring banks, and NPCI on
integrations, certifications, and compliance (PCI-DSS, RBI tokenization mandates, etc.)
• Partner with engineering to design scalable, secure payment flows (3DS, EMV 3-D Secure, network
tokenization, card vaulting)
• Collaborate with Sales, Risk, and Merchant Success teams to understand end-Merchant needs and translate
them into product requirements
• Analyze transaction data to identify drop-off points, decline reasons, and optimization opportunities in the card
payment funnel
• Stay ahead of regulatory changes (RBI guidelines) and industry trends affecting card payments in India
• Define and prioritize the product backlog, write detailed PRDs, and manage releases with engineering and QA
• Drive innovation within the Cards charter by co-developing new capabilities and pilots jointly with card
networks, regulators and banking partners
What We&#39;re Looking For
• 5+ years of product management experience, with a significant portion focused on Cards / card payments /
payment gateways
• Strong understanding of card payment rails — authorization, clearing, settlement, chargebacks, tokenization,
3DS/EMV 3DS
• Familiarity with RBI regulations relevant to card payments (tokenization, AFA, recurring mandates)
• Experience working with card networks, acquiring banks, or PSPs is a strong plus
• Data-driven approach with hands-on experience using SQL/analytics tools to drive decisions
• Excellent stakeholder management skills — comfortable working with engineering, business, banks, and
networks simultaneously
• Bachelor&#39;s degree required; MBA is a plus

• Prior experience at a fintech, PSP, payment gateway, or bank is highly preferred
• Experience with international card scheme certifications
• Exposure to fraud/risk systems in the card payment lifecycle
• Experience scaling products for high-volume, high-availability transaction systems
