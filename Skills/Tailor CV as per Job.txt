TAILOR CV AS PER JOB DESCRIPTION
=================================

[PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION]
Company: [COMPANY]
Role: [ROLE]
Link: [LINK]

Compare it against my ATS-Optimized Master CV (Avijit_Mukherjee_ATS_Optimized_CV) and produce a
tailored version as described below.

SOURCE OF TRUTH
---------------
Use profile/master_cv.md as the single canonical source for all claims, metrics, and evidence.
Every statement on the tailored CV must trace to a verified fact in that file or in
references/candidate-facts.md. Never invent or extrapolate.

SECTION STRUCTURE (ATS-SAFE — do not change headings or order)
-------------------------------
1. Name + Headline (tailored to JD keywords, still truthful)
2. Contact block (unchanged)
3. PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY (4-5 lines, rewritten around top 3-5 JD requirements)
4. CAREER HIGHLIGHTS (keep bullets most relevant to JD; drop weakest if space tight)
5. CORE SKILLS (re-ordered so most JD-relevant categories lead; never add false skills)
6. PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE (reverse-chronological, bullets reordered by JD relevance)
7. EDUCATION & CERTIFICATIONS
8. AWARDS & LANGUAGES

ATS FORMATTING RULES (must be preserved in every tailored output)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Single-column layout; no tables, text boxes, columns, icons, or graphics.
- Section headings exactly as above (all-caps in DOCX, title case in Markdown).
- Contact info in document body only (never in Word header/footer).
- Bullet label pattern: **Bold Label:** narrative — preserves ATS keyword indexing.
- Font: Calibri 9.5 pt body, 10.5 pt section headings, 10 pt role sub-headings (DOCX).
- Margins: 0.55 in top/bottom, 0.65 in left/right, US Letter 8.5 x 11 in.
- Target 2 pages; use 3 only if seniority evidence justifies it.
- File naming: CV_<Company>_<Role>_<YYYY-MM-DD>.docx

TAILORING INSTRUCTIONS
----------------------
1. Identify the 3-5 requirements the employer weights most heavily (look for repetition,
   ordering, and "must-have" language in the JD).
2. Rewrite the headline and Professional Summary around those requirements using only
   truthful evidence from the master CV.
3. Re-order experience bullets within each role so the most JD-relevant proof appears first.
4. Rewrite the top 3-4 bullets per relevant role using the Google XYZ formula:
   "Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]."
5. Mirror the JD's exact terminology only where it accurately maps to real experience.
6. Re-order CORE SKILLS categories so the most JD-relevant category leads.
7. Never add unsupported tools, certifications, metrics, or titles.
8. If the JD requires something genuinely lacking, state it in the Honest Gaps section
   below — do not conceal or pad around it.

OUTPUT REQUIRED
---------------
A. Final tailored CV in Markdown (preserving the ATS section structure above).
B. Change log table:
   | Section | Original text | Tailored text | Reason |
C. Keywords incorporated (list naturally; no stuffing).
D. Honest gaps and suggested ways to address them without fabrication.
E. Recruiter 10-second impression: what they will notice and any remaining concern.

SCORING & FIT ANALYSIS
-----------------------
Return:
1. Match score out of 100 with breakdown:
   - Must-have skills: score and evidence
   - Nice-to-have skills: score and evidence
   - Domain relevance
   - Seniority fit
   - Location / work-mode fit
2. Fit-gap table:
   | JD requirement | Evidence in master CV | Fit level | How to position truthfully | Gap? |
3. Top 5 most important keywords / themes to reflect in CV and cover letter.
4. Requirements not met — do not conceal or invent around them.
5. Recommendation: Apply / Apply with positioning / Skip — with reasoning.
