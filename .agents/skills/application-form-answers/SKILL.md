# Application Form Answers

Using the job description and the tailored CV, draft concise answers for typical application questions.

For each answer:
- Keep it between 50 and 150 words unless the form specifies otherwise.
- Use the STAR structure where a story is needed: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- Use XYZ framing in the Result.
- Use only facts from my master CV.
- Flag any question where I need to provide a new fact, metric, or example.

Cover these 10 question types:
1. Relevant experience (14 yrs in payments & platform delivery, $30M net revenue).
2. Product impact (Payments 2.0 multi-scheme launch on CyberSource).
3. Cross-functional collaboration (SMCC Japan delivery in 2 weeks vs. 6 weeks using agentic AI).
4. Handling ambiguity or conflicting priorities (Decomposing BASE I monolith into microservices without outage).
5. Data-driven decision-making (AI defect log analysis & settlement regeneration protecting $9.2M).
6. Stakeholder management (Managing enterprise banks across Europe, CEMEA, and US; leading 12 PMs).
7. Failure or lessons learned (Centralized 400-file release bottleneck -> automated on-demand self-service saving 8 FTEs/sprint).
8. Salary expectation ($100k-$200k USD equivalent / competitive senior market benchmark).
9. Notice period (Executive notice / immediate discussion).
10. Reason for leaving / interest in the company (Scaling core rails, international scheme expansion, AI industrialization).
