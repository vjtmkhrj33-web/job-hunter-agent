# Follow Up Drafter

Input: Company, Role, Date Applied, Contact Name (if known).

Draft:
1. Concise follow-up email to recruiter or hiring manager (maximum 120 words).
   - Role and application date
   - One specific, quantified proof point matching their platform needs
   - Polite request for an update or brief 15-minute conversation
   - Zero desperation, flattery, or repetitive claims
2. LinkedIn connection request (<300 characters).
3. LinkedIn direct message (<120 words).
4. LinkedIn search strategy: exact search strings to locate the hiring manager, Head of Product, or technical recruiter.
