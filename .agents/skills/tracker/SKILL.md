# Tracker

tracker.csv columns: company, role, link, location, sponsorship, score, status, applied_date, contact, follow_up_date, notes.
Statuses: shortlisted, applying, applied, referral-asked, recruiter-chat, interview, offer, rejected, withdrawn.
On update, change only the rows I name. For the weekly review report: counts per status, follow-ups due in the next 3 days, roles older than 10 days with no response, and the one thing most worth doing next.
