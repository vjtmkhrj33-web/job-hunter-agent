# Outreach drafting

For each APPLY role, write outreach.md with:
1. Referral ask to a first-degree contact (2-3 sentences, easy to say yes or no to).
2. Cold note to the hiring manager (under 80 words: one sharp observation about their product, one relevant proof point, one ask for a 15-minute chat or to be considered).
3. Note to a recruiter at the company (under 60 words, role link, 2 facts).
4. A follow-up message for day 5-7.
Rules: no flattery, no "I hope this finds you well", no fake familiarity. Suggest who to contact by role/title only, and I will find the actual people myself. Never contact anyone.
