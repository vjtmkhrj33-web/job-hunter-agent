# Pre-submit Quality Check

Before submitting an application, perform this final quality review:

Check:
1. **Accuracy**: Every claim traces directly back to the master CV or profile truth. Zero fabricated numbers.
2. **Job Alignment**: The CV directly addresses the employer's top technical and commercial priorities.
3. **ATS Compatibility**: Standard headings, single-column layout, no tables or graphics, clean typography, relevant keywords.
4. **Recruiter Readability**: Clear visual hierarchy, strong first-screen impact, no vague passive claims.
5. **Consistency**: Dates, job titles, employers, and certifications match across CV, cover letter, and LinkedIn.
6. **Tone**: Confident, direct, human; zero passive fluff or AI clichés ("spearheaded", "passionate", "synergy").
7. **Completeness**: No unresolved placeholder text (`[ADD METRIC]`, `[RESEARCH BEFORE SENDING]`), missing metrics, or unexplained gaps.

Output:
- Submission readiness score out of 100
- Critical fixes before applying
- Optional improvements
- Final approval verdict: READY / NOT READY
