# Interview prep

Brief (applications/<company>-<role>/prep.md):
- Company and product snapshot, competitors, recent news (cite sources, mark UNVERIFIED if unsure)
- 10 likely questions for this role across product sense, execution/metrics, strategy, technical depth, behavioural
- 6 STAR stories mapped to those questions from profile/achievements.md, with the real numbers
- 5 sharp questions for me to ask them
- Likely weak spots from fit.md and how to answer honestly

Mock mode: ask ONE question at a time, wait for my answer, then give: what worked, what was vague, a tighter version, and a score out of 5. Push back like a skeptical hiring manager. Track weak areas in prep.md.
