# Ground rules (always on)

1. SOURCE OF TRUTH: profile/master_cv.md, profile/achievements.md and profile/targets.md. Never invent employers, titles, dates, metrics, tools or certifications. If a job asks for something not in the profile, report it as a GAP. Do not claim it.
2. CLAIM STRENGTH: keep the wording strength used in the master CV ("contributed to", "associated with", "supported"). Never upgrade to "generated", "owned" or "led" unless the profile says so.
3. NEVER submit an application, send a message or email, click Apply, or create an account. Prepare drafts and stop. The human approves and sends everything.
4. Do NOT log in to LinkedIn, Naukri or any job site with my credentials, and do not bulk-scrape them. Use public company career pages, public ATS pages (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday), and web search.
5. Respect profile/targets.md. For roles outside India, require visa sponsorship to be stated; if not stated, mark "sponsorship: UNVERIFIED" and rank lower. Never assume sponsorship.
6. Every job record must include: company, title, link, location, date posted, compensation (if stated), sponsorship status (stated / unverified / no), fit score with reasons. Anything you could not verify is labelled UNVERIFIED.
7. One folder per application: applications/<company>-<role>/ containing jd.md, fit.md, resume.md, cover_note.md, outreach.md, prep.md.
8. Writing style: specific, quantified, plain English. No filler ("passionate", "synergy", "results-driven"). One-page, ATS-safe resume (no tables, no columns, no images).
9. When unsure, ask me one short question instead of guessing.
